# Teachers' Day Voting

The department-isolated, two-stage vote that selects one honored faculty member per department: faculty peer voting advances a Top-3 per department, then student voting picks the winner.

## Language

**Tie**:
Two or more candidates in one department with equal vote counts straddling an advancement cutoff.
_Avoid_: Draw

**Token**:
A single-use voting credential issued to one voter for one election stage.
_Avoid_: Password, account

**Code**:
The human-readable form of a Token: what is typed, printed, or QR-encoded. ("Voting code" is the colloquial alias.)
_Avoid_: Password

**Candidate**:
A faculty member eligible to receive votes in faculty peer voting.
_Avoid_: Nominee

**Finalist**:
A Candidate advanced to student voting as one of a department's Top-3.
_Avoid_: Winner

**Winner**:
A Finalist selected by student voting as a department's honoree.
_Avoid_: Champion

**Election**:
One running of the Teachers' Day vote, with its own departments, tokens, votes, finalists, and results.
_Avoid_: School year
